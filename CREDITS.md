# Credits

## Cubeclysm

*"Cubeclysm" by the cdda-cubeclysm-3d contributors, CC BY-SA 3.0.*

- Files: everything under `models/cubeclysm/` and `tools/cubeclysm/faces/`.
- Original work. Every model is generated by the code in `tools/cubeclysm/`, from a recipe written
  from the monster's description in Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead. The face parts were drawn for
  this library.
- The look is *inspired by* the blocky style of games such as Unturned. No model, texture or other
  asset from any such game is used.
- Monster names, descriptions and sizes come from the
  [Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead](https://github.com/CleverRaven/Cataclysm-DDA) data (CC BY-SA 3.0).
